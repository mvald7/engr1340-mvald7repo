# Addition of Two Integers

Addition is the process of combining two integers to find their sum.

## Example 1

* First integer: 5
* Second integer: 3
* Calculation: 5 + 3 = 8

The sum of 5 and 3 is 8.

## Example 2

* First integer: -6
* Second integer: 9
* Calculation: -6 + 9 = 3

The sum of -6 and 9 is 3.
